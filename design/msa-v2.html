Plate VI What an AXN does not claimobeli
three incisions · each a false equivalence cut

An AXN proves one narrow thing completely — and these three things it does not prove.

†

Identity ≠ authorship

Proves: that these exact bytes are these exact bytes. Does not prove: who made them. Authorship is recorded as a declaration and marked as such.

†

Witnessing ≠ notarization

Proves: when a file was witnessed here. Does not prove: when it was created. Nothing here is legal advice.

†

Registration ≠ perpetual hosting

Proves: that a position was allocated and recorded. Does not promise: storage without end. Storage terms are always explicit.
