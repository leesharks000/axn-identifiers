Plate V Free, forever — and what it does not claimFoundation
the promise is published and dated · the limits are stated in the same breath

Computing identifiers, stamping files, looking them up and verifying them is free, permanently. The registry is public and downloadable. Registry positions are never sold. Paid services buy human help around identifiers — never the identifier itself.

Identity ≠ authorship. It proves a file, not who made it.

Witnessing ≠ notarization. Timestamps prove when a file was witnessed, not created.

A registry entry ≠ perpetual hosting. Storage terms are always explicit.
