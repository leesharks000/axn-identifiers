the operation, in brief

Three layers: an identity kernel, a record address, and locations. The kernel is computed from your canonical bytes — nothing is uploaded to compute it.

i

Taken up

The identity kernel — SHA-256 of the canonical bytes, computed in your browser. No normalization, nothing transmitted.

ii

Inscribed

Six glyphs from the first six bytes of that hash, struck onto an added margin. Your canonical bytes are never altered.

iii

Witnessed

A record address — position and semantic family — in a public, exportable registry, plus locations naming where verified copies can be obtained.

Plate III The covenantstructural
the guarantee is in the design, not in our good behaviour

No promise of ours is load-bearing. Every line below follows from one fact: the identifier is computed from your bytes, so verifying it needs no permission, no service, and no one's continued goodwill — including ours.

2026-08-03

Free, permanently. Not free-for-now.

  • Computed, not granted. The kernel is the SHA-256 of your file. We do not issue it, and there is nothing for us to revoke.
  • Verifiable without us. Anyone holding the file recomputes the kernel themselves. No account, no key, no fee — there is nothing to charge for.
  • The registry is a static file in public version control, downloadable whole. No server's permission stands between a reader and the record.
  • Positions cannot be sold. A position is allocated by content order. Selling one would require an authority the system does not have.
  • The ending is specified. If this stops, the archive is published for download. An ending that was never planned for is just an erasure.
