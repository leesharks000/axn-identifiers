Plate II One object passing through statesthe operation
the operation, in brief

Three layers: an identity kernel, a record address, and locations. The kernel is computed from your canonical bytes — nothing is uploaded to compute it.

i

Taken up

The identity kernel — SHA-256 of the canonical bytes, computed in your browser. No normalization, nothing transmitted.

ii

Inscribed

Six glyphs from the first six bytes of that hash, struck onto an added margin. Your canonical bytes are never altered.

iii

Witnessed

A record address — position and semantic family — in a public, exportable registry, plus locations naming where verified copies can be obtained.
