What an AXN does not claim

Identity ≠ authorship. It proves a file, not who made it; authorship statements are recorded as declarations and marked as such.

Witnessing ≠ notarization. Timestamps prove when a file was witnessed, not created; nothing here is legal advice.

A registry entry ≠ perpetual hosting. Storage terms are always explicit.

If we disappear

Identifiers stay valid, because they're computed from your file — not granted by us.

The registry lives in public version control with full history, and if this service ever ends, the whole archive is offered for download during a stated wind-down. The constitution spells it out.

Errors are corrected by amendment and tombstone — never by silent deletion. A retired position keeps resolving; a withdrawn work is marked, not erased. The registry's history is itself part of the registry.
